Title: THE CURSE OF HOLLOW'S END
Author: Adventure Designer
Description: A Gothic horror adventure where you must lift the curse that plagues Hollow's End by confronting the vengeful spirit of the Widow and uncovering the dark secrets of the Hollow family.
Initial SKILL: 8
Initial STAMINA: 20
Initial LUCK: 9


Section 0: Background


Two hundred and forty-two years ago, on what should have been the happiest day of her life, Eliza Hollow was betrayed by her own family. Her father, Mordecai Hollow, murdered her beloved Thomas Ashford during their wedding ceremony and used forbidden necromancy to bind Eliza's spirit in eternal torment.


For generations, the Hollow family lived in fear as the curse grew stronger. Now the binding has finally broken, and the Widow has risen with an army of undead. The village of Hollow's End stands on the brink of destruction.


You are an adventurer who has answered Mayor Aldric Hollow's desperate call for help. Can you discover the Widow's true name, obtain the silver key, and bind her spirit before midnight strikes? The fate of Hollow's End rests in your hands.




Section 1


You stand at the entrance to Hollow's End as twilight deepens into true night, watching the last vestiges of daylight abandon this forsaken place to whatever horrors the darkness will bring. The transition from day to night feels faster here, more absolute, as if the sun itself is fleeing from what it knows lurks in the shadows. 


A weathered wooden sign marks the village boundary. The paint is peeling, the wood rotting from years of neglect, but you can still make out the faded letters: "HOLLOW'S END — POP. 247" 


Someone has scratched through the number with what looks disturbingly like dried blood, writing below: "232 and falling." 


The wind carries with it the scent of decay and turned earth. In the distance, you hear something that might be moaning... or might just be the wind itself moving through the abandoned buildings. It's impossible to tell, and somehow that uncertainty is worse than knowing. 


Three paths lie before you, each leading into the gathering gloom: 


To the left: A ramshackle tavern hunches against the growing darkness like a wounded animal seeking shelter. A swinging sign reads "The Weeping Willow" in faded gold letters that catch the last rays of dying light. Warm yellow light flickers in the windows—an inviting beacon in this dead village. Through the dusty glass, you can see shadows moving inside. Perhaps someone there has information about the curse. Perhaps they have ale to steady your nerves for what's to come. Or perhaps something darker waits behind that welcoming facade. 


Straight ahead: The cemetery gates stand twisted and torn open, as if something of immense strength had burst through from the inside out. The iron bars are bent outward at impossible angles, and deep gouges score the metal—claw marks from something that should not exist. Even from here, you can see fresh graves lying exposed, their earth disturbed, their wooden markers toppled, their occupants missing. The very heart of the curse, no doubt, and perhaps the quickest way to confront whatever evil plagues this place. But also the most dangerous. 


To the right: The Mayor's manor dominates the village square—a grand three-story building that must have been magnificent once but now looks haunted even before true darkness falls. Its windows are dark except for a few where candlelight flickers weakly, as if afraid to shine too brightly. You can see figures moving behind curtains, hurried and furtive. This is where Mayor Aldric Hollow lives, the man who sent the desperate call for help that brought you here. He will have information, resources, and perhaps the key to understanding the curse that stems from his own family's sins. 


Each path offers different opportunities and different dangers. The choice is yours, but choose wisely—your first decision may determine whether you live or die in the hours ahead.

The Widow stands in the center of the room, waiting for you. She's terrible and beautiful in equal measure—a tall figure in a wedding dress stained with 242 years of blood. Her face is hidden beneath a torn veil, but you can feel her hollow eyes fixed upon you with recognition and rage. 


"So," her voice echoes from everywhere and nowhere, layered with the screams of the dead, "another fool comes to stop me. Another hero seeking to prevent my righteous vengeance. Do you know what they did to me? Do you know what I suffered?"

"I was going to be HAPPY!" she shrieks, her form expanding, becoming more terrible. "I was going to have a LIFE! They murdered my husband! They CHAINED my spirit! They left me in darkness for TWO HUNDRED AND FORTY-TWO YEARS! And you... you dare try to imprison me again?"

THE PROPER BINDING RITUAL: 


You hold up the SILVER KEY with one hand while your other hand clutches whatever protective amulets you carry to your chest. Drawing upon every ounce of courage and strength you possess, you shout with absolute authority: 


"ELIZA HOLLOW! I bind you by your true name, by this silver seal forged for spirit-binding, and by the ancient laws that govern the dead! Your curse ends TONIGHT!"

Her clawed hands become delicate fingers

A single tear rolls down her cheek, leaving a trail of silver light. 


"Thank you," she whispers, her voice no longer the inhuman wail that chilled your blood. It's simply the voice of a woman who has been in pain for too long. "Thank you for remembering my name. Thank you for setting me free." 


"Where is Thomas?" she asks, looking around as if seeing clearly for the first time in centuries. 


And then she sees him. 


A second ghostly form materializes—Thomas Ashford, waiting patiently for 242 years, ready to finally take his bride's hand. He wears wedding clothes that are no longer stained with blood, and his eyes are filled with love. 


Eliza's face transforms with joy so pure it hurts to witness. She reaches out, and Thomas takes her hand. Where their fingers touch, golden light blooms—warm light that fills the room and drives away every shadow. 


"My love," she breathes. "You waited." 


"Forever," he replies. "I would have waited forever." 


The chains binding Eliza shatter into a thousand motes of silver light that float upward like fireflies ascending to heaven. But she doesn't attack. She simply stands there, holding Thomas's hand, looking into his eyes with all the love that death could not destroy. 


Together, they begin to fade. 


"Peace," Eliza whispers, looking back at you. "I can finally find peace. Tell them... tell the people of Hollow's End... I forgive them. Life is too short, and death too long, for hatred." 


And then they're gone. 


The manor shudders and groans. The walls begin to crumble—the entire structure was being held together by the Widow's rage. Now that she's at peace, the building is finally succumbing to its 242 years of abandonment.   
You run through collapsing corridors, down crumbling stairs, across floors that fall away into darkness. You burst through the front doors just as the entire structure implodes behind you, collapsing into rubble and dust. 


You tumble down the steps and roll to a stop in the overgrown garden, gasping for breath. 


And then you notice: the sky is lightening. Dawn is breaking. 


The first golden rays of sunrise paint the sky in shades of pink and orange and gold. It's the most beautiful sunrise you've ever seen, made more beautiful by the simple fact that you're alive to see it. 


Down in the cemetery, you watch the undead collapsing. Zombies fall back into their graves. Ghouls dissolve to dust. Skeletal warriors crumble to bones. The curse has been lifted. 


After 242 years, Hollow's End is finally free.
