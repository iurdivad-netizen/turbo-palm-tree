**0**
Introduction

Welcome brave adventurer! This is the introduction to your quest.

**ABOUT THIS ADVENTURE:**
This adventure uses Markdown bold formatting for section numbers.
When you see **0**, **1**, **2**, etc., these are section numbers
formatted with double asterisks (which creates bold text in Markdown).

**THE STORY:**
You are a hero seeking the legendary Crystal of Power. It lies deep
within the Caverns of Doom, guarded by fearsome creatures and deadly
traps. Your courage, skill, and luck will determine your fate.

**HOW TO PLAY:**
You'll see a "Start Adventure" button below. Click it to begin your
quest at section **1**. Throughout your journey, make choices that
will lead you to victory or defeat.

May fortune favor you on your adventure!

**1**
The Cavern Entrance

You stand before the mouth of a dark cavern. Ancient runes are carved
into the stone archway above the entrance. The air is cold and carries
a faint smell of sulfur.

A weathered sign reads: "Enter at your own peril. Many have entered,
few have returned."

If you want to enter the cavern, turn to **2**.
If you want to search for another entrance, go to **3**.

**2**
Into the Darkness

You step through the entrance and descend into the cavern. The passage
slopes downward, lit by glowing fungi on the walls. You hear water
dripping in the distance.

Suddenly, you see two paths ahead.

To take the left path, turn to **4**.
To take the right path, turn to **5**.

**3**
The Hidden Path

You search around the cavern entrance and discover a hidden path behind
some bushes. This route looks safer and better lit.

Following this path, you discover the Crystal of Power resting on a
pedestal! There are no guards or traps here.

You have won! Congratulations on finding the secret route!

**4**
The Underground Lake

The left path leads to an underground lake. The water is crystal clear
and surprisingly warm. You notice glowing crystals at the bottom.

You reach into the water and retrieve a beautiful crystal. It's the
Crystal of Power! But it was a trap - the crystal was fake, and you're
now stuck in quicksand that appeared beneath your feet.

You have died. Game over.

**5**
The Guardian's Chamber

The right path opens into a vast chamber. In the center, on a golden
pedestal, sits the Crystal of Power! But between you and the crystal
stands a massive stone guardian that comes to life as you enter.

The guardian speaks: "Answer my riddle, or face your doom!"

Fortunately, you know the answer to the ancient riddle. The guardian
steps aside, and you claim the Crystal of Power.

You have won! Victory is yours, brave adventurer!
