Title: Marker Test Adventure
Author: Test Author
Description: Test adventure demonstrating inline marker syntax
Initial SKILL: 8
Initial STAMINA: 20
Initial LUCK: 9

Section 1
The Cave Entrance

You stand before a dark cave entrance. Inside, you can see flickering torchlight.

A {combat: GOBLIN GUARD | skill=6 | stamina=7 | onVictory=2 | onDefeat=99} blocks the entrance!

Section 2
Victory!

You defeat the guard and enter the cave. On the ground you find a {item: rusty sword | type=weapon | bonus=+1 skill} and {treasure: 25 gold coins}.

{action: +2 stamina | You rest and recover}

Will you {choice: 3 | Go deeper into the cave} or {choice: 4 | if: skill>7 | Try to sneak past the next room}?

Section 3
The Treasure Room

You find a massive treasure chest! Inside are:
- {treasure: 100 gold coins}
- {item: healing potion | type=potion | restore=+4 stamina}
- {item: magic amulet | type=other | bonus=+1 luck}

This is the end of your adventure. You win!

Section 4
The Stealth Path

You successfully sneak past the guards using your skills!

You find a {item: lockpick | type=other} hidden in a crevice.

{action: testLuck | success=5 | fail=6}

Section 5
Lucky!

You were lucky! The trap doesn't trigger. Turn to 3.

Section 6
Unlucky!

You were unlucky! The trap triggers. {action: -3 stamina}

If you survive, turn to 3.

Section 99
Game Over

You have been defeated. Your adventure ends here.
