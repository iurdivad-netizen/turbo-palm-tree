This is the introduction section. In many Fighting Fantasy and gamebook
imports, section numbers appear as standalone numbers on their own line,
without the word "section" or any punctuation.

This format is very common in:
- PDF exports from original books
- OCR scanned gamebooks
- Text files from various sources

The parser now recognizes this format automatically. When you load this
adventure, you'll see this introduction first with a "Start Adventure"
button. Click it to begin at section 1.

1

You stand in a dimly lit corridor. The stone walls are damp and covered
with ancient moss. Two passages branch off ahead of you.

If you take the left passage, turn to 2
If you take the right passage, turn to 3

2

You follow the left passage and discover a hidden treasure room! Gold
coins and jewels sparkle in the torchlight. You carefully gather the
treasure and find an exit that leads to safety.

You have won! Congratulations on your successful adventure!

3

You take the right passage, but it leads to a dead end. As you turn
around, you hear a rumbling sound. The ceiling begins to collapse!

You narrowly escape with your life, but you must abandon the quest
for now. Perhaps you'll have better luck next time.

Your adventure ends here. The end.

999

This is the traditional "death" ending section. If you had taken a
wrong turn or failed in combat, you might have ended up here.

Your adventure ends in defeat. Game over.
